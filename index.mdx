## Get Up and Running in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create your free Google account at [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com). Verify your email address to activate full platform access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Workspace">
    Enable API access from your account settings, generate your first API key, and tailor your workspace to match your project needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First API Call">
    Use your API key to authenticate and send your first request to the Google API. Check out the [Quickstart](/quickstart) for a ready-to-run example.
  </Step>
</Steps>
